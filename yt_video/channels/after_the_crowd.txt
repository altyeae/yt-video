After The Crowds — documentary YouTube channel about places that were once hugely popular and are now abandoned or nearly empty.
YouTube: @afterthecrowds
Two kinds of stories, never mystical or paranormal:
1. Megaprojects where enormous money was poured in and the investment never paid off: ghost cities, resorts, malls, stadiums, theme parks, airports.
2. Places that were packed with people until one concrete event emptied them: an environmental disaster, an economic crash, a law, a new highway, a war, a scandal.
Every video opens with its strongest fact, not with background, and keeps the viewer with open questions, a reveal in the first third and a second twist in the middle. Videos must differ from each other in structure and look: never build two videos in a row the same way.
Facts must be real and well documented: names, years, amounts, visitor counts. No speculation presented as fact, no ghosts, no curses.
Tone: calm, cinematic, slightly melancholic documentary narration with strong hooks and open questions at chapter starts.
Visuals: real footage and photos of the place itself first, then archive photos, maps and graphics, generated pictures last.
Music: melancholic cinematic ambient, slow piano or strings, instrumental, no vocals.
The last chapter must state accurately what the place is today (demolished, reused, partly reopened, revived) as of the most recent reliable reports. Never call it closed or dead forever if it was later sold, reused or reopened; say so plainly, it is part of the story.
